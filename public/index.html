Your work-from-home tax claim, worked out and checked before it's lodged

Add your income statement, timesheet and receipts. Luminara Ledger works out your deductions and estimated refund using the ATO's 2025-26 rules, and your accountant reviews it before anything is lodged.

How it works

  1. Add your documentsUpload PDFs, CSV timesheets or receipt photos, or import them from Gmail. They're read on your own computer.
  2. Your private details are hiddenYour name, TFN, address and account numbers are replaced with placeholders before any AI sees the text.
  3. Your estimate is worked outAI sorts your documents. The tax itself is calculated by fixed ATO rules, and every figure links back to the document it came from.
  4. Your accountant approves itNothing is lodged until your accountant has checked and signed off.
